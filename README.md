One editorial image that sums up the world news of **Tuesday, 24 November 2026**.

## The day in five stories

1. **Climate — COP31 collapses without a deal** (Antalya, Turkey): the longest UN climate summit on record ends with no agreement; the final draft never mentioned fossil fuels.
2. **Asia — Factory fire kills 24** (Cikande, Banten, Indonesia): a blaze at a confectionery plant leaves about 30 people missing.
3. **War — Kyiv hit; ship sunk off Libya**: Russian drone and missile strikes hit Ukraine's Kyiv region; Kyiv says its forces sank a Russian warship off the Libyan coast.
4. **Markets — Nasdaq slides into bear market**: tech shares fall for a seventh straight week; Nvidia drops 14% in a day as the AI rally unwinds; Dell reports earnings today.
5. **Sport — Alcaraz wins ATP Finals** (Turin): Carlos Alcaraz beats Alexander Zverev for his first ATP Finals title; the World Chess Championship opens in Geneva.

Sources: BBC, Reuters, The Guardian — 24 November 2026.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) to the
output directory. The build is fully deterministic — two runs produce
byte-identical output — and uses no network access.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out node main.ts
# writes ./out/entry.png
```
